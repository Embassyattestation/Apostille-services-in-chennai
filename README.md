## When might you need an apostille?

An apostille is used to authenticate the origin of a public document for presentation in another country that is part of the Hague Apostille Convention. It confirms the authenticity of the signature, the capacity in which the person signing acted, and the identity of the seal or stamp.

Apostille may be requested for documents used in:

- Overseas employment and professional registration
- Admission to foreign universities
- Immigration and family-related procedures
- International business and commercial transactions
- Legal or administrative matters abroad

The receiving institution determines which documents it requires and whether any additional steps are necessary.

## Identify the right process for your document

The authentication route depends on the document’s category and issuing authority.

### Academic records

Degree certificates, diplomas, and other educational records may need verification by the relevant state authority or another designated authority before MEA apostille. Requirements can vary based on where the certificate was issued.

### Civil and personal records

Birth certificates, marriage certificates, affidavits, and other personal documents may require preliminary authentication by the appropriate state or local authority. Check that the details match your supporting identification.

### Business and commercial paperwork

Commercial documents used for overseas business may follow a different verification route, which can involve a Chamber of Commerce or another designated authority. Confirm the requirements for the specific document and destination.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is an apostille?

An apostille is an official certificate that authenticates the origin of a public document for use in another country covered by the Hague Apostille Convention.

### Can documents issued in another state be apostilled in Chennai?

The appropriate process depends on the issuing authority and the document’s preliminary authentication requirements. Confirm the correct route before submitting the document.

### Do educational certificates need state verification?

Educational certificates may require verification by the relevant state authority or another designated authority before MEA apostille. The exact requirements depend on the certificate.

### Is apostille the same as embassy attestation?

No. Apostille is generally used for documents intended for Convention member countries. Other destinations may require embassy or consular legalisation.

### Does apostille guarantee acceptance abroad?

No. Apostille authenticates the document’s origin, but the receiving institution may also require translations, supporting records, or a specific document format.
